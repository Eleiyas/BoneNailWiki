**Yinchen Zhang** is the main protagonist across the "Monster Dream" mythos, although he is only named and visually presented in [[Blood Flowers]]. 

# Profile
Yinchen is a young man who can reasonably be assumed to be in his mid-to-late 20s, based on the dates of events depicted in _Blood Flowers_ and [[The Piper of Dawn]].

Although Yinchen was adopted by [[Ximu Zhang]] as a child, his biological origins remain unknown. His true nature is similarly unclear, with several games hinting at a connection to divinity and the supernatural.

He currently works for the [[Meteorite Association]] under [[Nix]], being regarded as their top meteorite hunter, and _Nix_ wanting him to step-up to lead the association in the future.

# Story
Yinchen was adopted by [[Ximu Zhang]] at a young age, although the circumstances surrounding his discovery and adoption remain largely unknown. Even as a child, Yinchen possessed an unusual ability to perceive the supernatural while retaining his sanity (SAN), something that appears to distinguish him from ordinary humans. The nature of these abilities, and whether they were innate or acquired, remains unclear.

As a child, it is stated that Yinchen was corrupted by a meteorite, leading to the creation of a [[Soul Stone]] by his adoptive father, in order to protect his existence. However, the nature of this corruption, and whether it was responsible for his supernatural abilities or merely altered them or contained them, is unknown.

During his school years, he becomes acquainted with a young [[Jimo Li]], and during a later trip to see his adoptive father at [[Guanghua College]], he becomes minimally acquainted with **Ye Ying** ([[Little Leaf]]), speaking to her through one of the classroom doors while she is trapped inside during a meteorite contamination incident. 

Although there is no explicitly stated date, at some point between his school years and the present, _Ximu Zhang_ disappeared and was presumed deceased, leaving his assets and a property to Yinchen in a will, with the property not initially being of any interest due to its location and state of decay.

During adulthood, while working at the association, Yinchen is sent to the [[Bai City Geological Institute]] in order to procure several meteorite samples, notably [[CP-06]]. This leads him to become intertwined in the events surrounding the Blood Tree Plague and the various supernatural occurrences connected to meteorites and Bai City. During the course of these events, he travels back in time and inhabits his younger self, although the exact circumstances and implications of this event remain unclear. While possessing his younger self, he finds-out about his adoptive father's research, notably that _Ximu_ had found something dangerous that worried him. Likewise, Yinchen experienced his cousin [[Jie Bai]]'s descent into madness as he delved into alchemy and the supernatural in order to protect his daughter. Yinchen later travels even further back in time to 1899 Qing Dynasty China, possessing the body of a comatose [[Tuizhi Bai]] in the process, and becoming acquainted with [[Linyu Mei]] and an earlier incarnation of [[Vulkan K]] named "Mr Kai". Through experiencing these events first-hand, along with finding the **Notes of LanZhuTang** in the present-day, the story of the Bai Family Curse and the Blood Tree Plague are laid-bare, and the mystery of his cousin's death is revealed. Upon returning to the present, Yinchen is then witness to the self-sacrifice of his niece, [[Sue Bai]], who had travelled back and experienced the events of 1899 alongside him, becoming the new "Goddess" of the Blood Tree, in order to stop the plague-event from repeating once more.

Following these events, and the passing of _Ximu Zhang_, his cousin _Jie Bai_, his niece _Sue Bai_, alongside a mounting unexplained debt, Yinchen heads to the location where he is told that the final will of his adoptive father is being read. This causes him to inherit the [[Yog-Sothoth's Yard]], where he becomes involved in the affairs of its inhabitants and the supernatural forces surrounding the property. While working alongside his newly hired staff members _Little Leaf_, [[Yevna]], [[Haa Lou Ling]], and [[Tlipoca]], he uncovers the truth of _Ximu_'s disappearance into [[Black Star Town]], the secret and shady dealings between [[HOBA]], the [[Aurora Association]], and the [[Spirits Authority]], along with his connection to the **Lord of the Gate**. During the "Final Carnival", Yinchen, with the might of his staff members, and help from _Nix_ and the _Meteorite Association_, decides to put an end to the charade, leading to his ascension to the "Gate of Truth", coming to terms with the fact that he is likely an avatar or offspring of **Yog-Sothoth** itself. Upon rejecting the offer proposed by the being, Yinchen becomes a timeless and ageless being, split across all timelines.

**NOTE:** **The events of Yog-Sothoth's Yard can lead to a variety of different endings, with Yinchen's fate differing depending on the choices made throughout the game.**

**NOTE2:** **Given the possibility of infinite timelines, the events of The Piper of Dawn do not necessarily follow chronologically after the Yard. If anything, the lack of mentions of characters specific to the Yard suggests that the game does take place in an alternate timeline.**

Following the events at the **Bai City Geological Institute** and the resolution of the Blood Tree Plague, _Nix_ informs Yinchen of a place in Europe known as [[Diamond City]], where a series of unusual events have been taking place, potentially as a result of a meteorite situated nearby. Upon arriving in the city, Yinchen finds himself stripped of his prior memories and trapped in the **Golden Cage**, a mysterious establishment where he is made to play blackjack alongside [[Hansel]] and [[Doctor Gadwin]], while facing an entity claiming to be **Death**. Sensing that something is amiss, Yinchen uses his supernatural abilities to perceive the entity's true condition, discovering that it is bound to the Golden Cage and has almost entirely exhausted its power. Recognising Yinchen's ability to see through its circumstances, the entity offers him its remaining power in exchange for granting him one final wish. Following this encounter, Yinchen assumes the title of **Alchemist** and inherits the **Alchemist's Workshop** in Diamond City. Over the course of his first month in the city, Yinchen quickly gained renown with the locals, while also hiring [[Sissi]] as a helper, and discovering that the local stray black cat was actually **Jimo Li** who had been following him since his school years. After becoming slightly closer to the [[Heim Guard]], he meets [[Gretel]], who retains some of her memories from prior to Diamond City, prompting the duo to investigate. He also meets the skeleton of the **Former Alchemist** in the workshop's loft who does not explain much, but gives Yinchen the beginnings of the **Tree of Creation**. After meeting [[Luna]], who disappeared as quickly as she appeared, the Workshop is attacked by a monster, but the whole ordeal is covered-up, with the blame being placed on Yinchen. He is lynched and placed as a sacrifice to the Goddess, [[Aurora]]. However, instead of being slain himself, the powers of Death awaken inside him and he slaughters the township, only to find himself back at the workshop as if nothing happened. After multiple monthly rewinds, and slowly regaining his memories, it is uncovered that **Diamond City** has been in this state of repeating the same month since 1899 due to a pact that [[Grand Duke Ludwig]] signed with [[The Piper]], and that the **Former Alchemist** is actually a prior version of Yinchen himself who failed to complete the **Tree of Creation** and escape from [[The Witch]]. It is likewise revealed that the "Death" he had dealt with in the _Golden Cage_ was actually a part of [[Thanatos]], another member from the _Meteorite Association_ who had infiltrated Diamond City. While working alongside **Sissi**, [[Nene]], **Jimo Li**, **Gretel**, **Aurora**, **Luna**, **Thanatos**, and the various Factions of Diamond City, Yinchen uncovers the maelstroms flowing beneath the Earth, which keep the city in its state of permanence. One of these maelstroms causes him, and _Luna_, to travel back to the eve of the millennium in 1999, where he meets _Nix_, _Ximu Zhang_ and _Jie Bai_ (Kyle) in an internet cafe. In order to fully break the hold that The Piper has on Diamond City, Yinchen ends up completing his Tree of Creation, which, in conjunction with Luna breaking her ties to the [[Skyseed]], causes the magic encompassing the city to unravel. 

**NOTE:** **Although the majority of The Piper of Dawn's endings happen at the end of Chapter 5 with the face-off with The Piper, they are split between Yinchen remaining in Diamond City with the rewind still in effect, having him lead the people out into the modern era, or him instead taking on the power of Death he inherited, ending up gaining differing levels of divinity. There is also a worse ending.**
