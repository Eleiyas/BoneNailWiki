# Yog-Sothoth's Yard

<img class="gamelogo" src="/assets/logo/YSYLogo.png">

**Yog-Sothoth's Yard** is a [Lovecraftian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovecraftian_horror)-inspired [Dating Sim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dating_sim) and [Business simulation game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_simulation_game), created by [Bone Nail Studio](https://x.com/BoneNailStudio) and published by [Shochiku Japan](https://game.shochiku.co.jp/). It was released for Windows via [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/) on 20th October 2023.

The game takes place in an alternate reality where spirits and other supernatural beings are commonplace. The protagonist inherits a villa burdened with debt, aiming to turn it into a blossoming hotel business, becoming close with the diverse staff, and uncovering dark-dealings along the way.

It is the second game within the "Monster Dream" mythos.

## Story
The player assumes the role of the Protagonist ([[Yincheng Zhang]]), the adopted son of renowned meteorite researcher [[Ximu Zhang]], commonly known as “the Professor”. Following Zhang's sudden death, the protagonist inherits an abandoned villa situated in an isolated and seemingly inaccessible location.

In order to repay the debts attached to the property, the protagonist converts the villa into the Yog-Sothoth's Yard, a hotel catering to an increasingly unusual collection of guests. As new employees join the staff and strange phenomena begin to surround the hotel, the protagonist becomes involved in mysteries concerning the Professor's research, the villa's history and the supernatural forces connected to both.

## Gameplay
**Yog-Sothoth's Yard** combines [Visual Novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_novel) storytelling with hotel-management and business-simulation mechanics.

The game progresses through a week-based calendar. Story scenes and dialogue choices are presented in a visual-novel format, while the player spends Action Points managing the hotel, gathering resources and interacting with its staff.

Hotel management includes constructing and upgrading rooms, accommodating guests, preparing food, performing staff operations and balancing expenses against the hotel's income. New characters introduce additional facilities and mechanics as the story progresses.

The player's choices influence character **Affinity**, the protagonist's **SAN** and **EVIL** values, and the events and endings that become available. Although the main story follows a set schedule, players are given considerable freedom in how they develop the hotel and spend their time between story events.
